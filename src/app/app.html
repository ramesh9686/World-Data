Privacy Statement & Data Protection

World Explorer GIS & Telemetry Suite

🔒 1. Zero Tracking & Local Storage Integrity

Your geographic searches, viewport navigation, and game scores are processed 100% locally inside your browser. No personal identifiable information (PII) is tracked, sold, or transmitted to external servers.

🛰️ 2. Public Live Telemetry Sockets

Real-time AIS ship positions, Foreign Exchange rates, and World Bank indicators are requested strictly via secure encrypted HTTPS/WSS endpoints. Only public telemetry telemetry streams are consumed.

🍪 3. Cookies & Session Preference Compliance

Session parameters (such as theme preferences, audio mute states, and optional AIS Stream API keys) are persisted locally in browser localStorage and never shared with 3rd-party advertisers.

✅ Fully Compliant with Open GIS & Web Privacy Standards
